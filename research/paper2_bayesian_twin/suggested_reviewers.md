# Suggested reviewers – roles and selection criteria

No names are given here on purpose. The author must select real reviewers himself (with the supervisor), check their
recent publications and e-mail addresses on the publisher's or university's web pages, and make sure that none of the
conflicts listed below applies. Never suggest a person only because a tool proposed the name.

## Profiles to look for (one or two persons per profile)

| # | Profile | Why this expertise is needed | Where to find candidates |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Researcher in **input uncertainty / Bayesian methods in stochastic simulation** | judges the conjugate models, nested Monte Carlo, variance decomposition and the statistical evaluation | authors of recent articles on input uncertainty in simulation (e.g. in *Operations Research*, *INFORMS Journal on Computing*, *ACM TOMACS*, Winter Simulation Conference); see refs. on input uncertainty in the manuscript |
| 2 | Researcher in **digital twins of manufacturing systems** (system level, not product level) | judges the twin concept, the frozen-prediction protocol and the positioning against twin literature | recent first or corresponding authors in *Journal of Manufacturing Systems*, *Robotics and Computer-Integrated Manufacturing*, *IJPR*, *CIRP Annals* on digital twins with simulation and data updating |
| 3 | Researcher in **production ramp-up or commissioning** | judges the realism of the virtual ramp-up (shape, magnitudes) and the field-validation protocol | authors of ramp-up studies in *Production Planning & Control*, *International Journal of Production Economics*, Procedia CIRP (CMS conference) |
| 4 | Researcher in **performance evaluation of FMS / manufacturing system modelling** | judges the reduced-order DES, the bottleneck metric and the case | authors in *Flexible Services and Manufacturing Journal*, *IISE Transactions*, *Journal of Manufacturing Systems* on FMS/flow-line modelling |
| 5 (optional) | Researcher in **probabilistic forecasting / forecast evaluation** | judges interval scores, calibration and the comparison with persistence baselines | authors in forecasting journals (e.g. *International Journal of Forecasting*) who work on proper scoring rules |

## Selection criteria

- active in the last five years (at least two relevant journal articles since 2021);
- at least one reviewer from outside the author's country and, if possible, one from a country at a similar stage of
  industrial development (understands the motivation);
- mix of methodological (profiles 1, 5) and application (profiles 2–4) expertise;
- holds a doctorate and an academic or research position (journals usually verify this);
- institutional e-mail address (no private addresses).

## Exclusion criteria (conflicts of interest)

- co-authors of the authors in the last 3–5 years, the supervisor's own group, members of the thesis committee;
- persons at the authors' institution or with a joint project / funding in the last 3–5 years;
- close personal relationships;
- authors of the companion papers or anyone who has already read the thesis in an official role;
- editors of the target journal (they cannot act as reviewers for it).

## Opposed reviewers

Only if there is a concrete reason (e.g. a direct competitor on the same case). Give a short, neutral reason.
