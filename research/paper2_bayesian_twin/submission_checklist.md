# Submission checklist – Paper 2 (uncertainty-aware digital twin with sequential Bayesian updating)

## 1. Target journal and why

**Primary: *Journal of Manufacturing Systems* (JMS, Elsevier).** Reasons: (i) JMS publishes system-level digital-twin,
simulation and data-driven manufacturing-system papers — the core of this manuscript; (ii) the contribution is a
twin *method* plus a simulation evaluation, which fits JMS better than the operations-research focus of the
*International Journal of Production Research* (IJPR would expect a stronger optimisation or planning contribution,
which belongs to the staging paper); (iii) the closest related works cited (digital-twin reviews, live fitting of
simulation inputs, design vs. commissioned system) are in the JMS / manufacturing-systems community.
**Alternative (open access, SCIE): *Machines* or *Applied Sciences* (MDPI)** — faster, article processing charge,
structure "Introduction – Materials and Methods – Results – Discussion – Conclusions", shorter abstract (check the
current MDPI instructions; usually about 200 words) and MDPI reference style. If the paper is moved there, re-format
the abstract and section titles; the content can stay.

**Note on the guide for authors.** The official JMS guide page (sciencedirect.com) could not be opened by the
automated tools (HTTP 403). The requirements below were taken from search-engine extracts of the official guide on
14–15 Nov 2026. **Open the current guide in a browser and re-check every item before submission.**

4. **Generative-AI statement**: the manuscript contains a draft. Elsevier requires a declaration of the tool and
   its purpose; AI tools cannot be authors, and AI-generated images are not allowed except as part of the research
   design. Check (a) the current JMS/Elsevier policy, (b) your university's doctoral regulations on AI use, and
   (c) that the statement is true and complete (which tool, for what). Keep a record of what was AI-assisted.
5. **Plagiarism / text-overlap check against the thesis**: the manuscript was written as new prose, but it is based on
   thesis chapters 4, 6 and 9. Run the similarity check of your university (iThenticate / Turnitin) with the thesis
   and paper 1 included, and rewrite any long overlapping passages. Elsevier states that publication of an academic
   thesis is **not** regarded as prior publication (Elsevier, "Clarification of our policy on prior publication",
   2016; check the current JMS guide). Disclose the thesis in the cover letter (already done) and, if the thesis is
   deposited in a repository before acceptance, mention this to the editor. Check your university's rules on
   whether the thesis may contain the published article (most allow it with a citation of the journal version and
   the publisher's permission/licence).
6. **Companion papers**: keep the "in preparation" citations consistent with the other four manuscripts; if one is
   submitted first, cite it as "submitted to …" and give the editor a copy. Do not submit two papers with
   overlapping results to the same or different journals at the same time.
7. **Main configuration vs. ablation**: the paper reports honestly that the ablation P10D (no forgetting of station
   parameters, with discrepancy) forecasts output better than the main configuration P07D. Keep this; reviewers will
   value it. Do not change the main configuration after seeing the results.
