## B. Author to-dos before submission

1. **Author data**: names, affiliations, e-mails, ORCID, CRediT roles (Section "Author contributions").
2. **Funding statement**: real funder and grant number, or "no funding".
3. **References**: resolve every `[verify]` (Browne 1984, Solberg 1977, Suri & Hildebrant 1984, Seidmann et al. 1987, Kampa
   et al. 2026, Molnár et al. 2021, van Ginkel et al. 2025, Purwaningsih et al. 2025, Modbus 2012); add DOIs for Stecke 1983,
   Little 1961, Sethi & Sethi 1990, Koren et al. 1999, Negahban & Smith 2014 from the publisher pages. Read at least the
   abstracts of all cited works you have not read yourself.
4. **Generative-AI statement**: adapt the draft to what was really done (which tool, for which tasks, what you checked).
   Springer: LLM use must be documented in the Methods; pure copy editing need not be declared; you remain fully
   responsible for all content.
5. **Overlap / text recycling**: run the manuscript through the university's similarity checker (iThenticate / Turnitin)
   against the thesis (chapters 4, 8, 9) and the guide "Doctor paper/54_fms_cell_controller.md". The text was written
   new, but tables and numbers come from the same work – target < 15 % overall similarity and no copied paragraphs.
6. **Thesis-reuse policy**: the thesis is not yet published. Springer Nature states that a thesis / preprint is normally
   not prior publication and that authors may reuse their published article in their thesis – **confirm the current
   Springer Nature policy and the university's rules for a "thesis by publication"** and state in the cover letter that
   the paper is based on the doctoral work. If the thesis is published in a repository before submission, cite it.
7. **Companion papers**: four further manuscripts from the same doctoral project are in preparation (review; Bayesian
   twin updating; Modbus/HTTP interface; staged investment). Make sure no table or figure appears in two papers; cite a
   companion paper only once it is accepted or available as a preprint (unpublished works must not be in the reference
   list – at present they are mentioned only in the text and in the cover letter).
8. **No simultaneous submission**: submit this paper to one journal only; keep the alternative journals (JMS, IJPR,
   Machines) for a possible resubmission after a decision.
9. **Numbers**: every number is labelled in the text as design estimate, calculated or measured on the virtual plant.
   If real data from the factory become available before submission, add them as a separate subsection – never replace
   simulated values silently.
10. **Reproduce once more** on a quiet PC: `python code/mva_models.py`, `python code/batch.py 4`, `python code/analyse.py`,
    `python code/make_figures.py`, `python code/refs.py` (see `code/README` section at the end of this checklist).
11. **Language**: ask a fluent English speaker or the university's editing service for a final language check (AI
    copy editing is allowed without declaration, but you must check the result).
12. **Figures**: convert to EPS/TIFF and check them printed in grey scale.

## C. How the package was produced (for the author)

| Step | Command (from `research/paper5_fms_cell_design/code`) | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Analytical models | `python mva_models.py` | `data/mva_results.json` |
| Virtual-plant experiments (61 runs, ~100 min with 8 in parallel, ports 17500–17999) | `python batch.py 8` | `data/raw/*.json` |
| KPIs and confidence intervals | `python analyse.py` | `data/runs.csv`, `data/summary.json` |
| Figures | `python make_figures.py` | `figures/*.svg` |
| Manuscript with numbered references | `python refs.py` | `manuscript.md` |
| PDF / DOCX | from the project root: `python research/tools/build_paper.py research/paper5_fms_cell_design` | `build/` |

Note on validity: a first batch with the production Modbus time-out (0.3 s) on a heavily loaded host produced false
OFFLINE detections (devices declared offline for 5 s wall time = 100 s plant time) and throughputs 30–40 % too low. It
is kept in `data/raw_invalid_t03/` and is **not** used in the paper; the valid batch uses a 2 s harness time-out and every
run is checked for zero OFFLINE events (Section 5.2 of the manuscript).
