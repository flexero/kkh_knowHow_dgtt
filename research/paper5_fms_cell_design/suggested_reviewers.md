# Suggested reviewers – selection criteria (paper 5)

No names are proposed here on purpose. The author must choose real reviewers himself, check them in Scopus / Web of
Science, and enter them in the submission system. Never copy reviewer names from an AI tool without checking that the
person exists, works in the field and has a current institutional e-mail address.

## Profiles to look for (choose 3–5, at least two countries, none from the author's own institution)

| # | Profile | Why this profile fits the manuscript | How to find candidates |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Researcher in **analytical performance models of manufacturing systems** (closed queueing networks, mean value analysis, CONWIP / pallet-limited systems) | can judge Sections 4 and 5: the exact multi-server MVA, the blocking-aware approximation (model B), the composite estimate and their error against the virtual plant | Scopus: TITLE-ABS-KEY("mean value analysis" AND (manufacturing OR "flexible manufacturing")) and ("closed queueing network" AND pallet), last 10 years; authors with ≥ 2 papers |
| 2 | Researcher in **FMS scheduling and dispatching rules / real-time control of FMS** | can judge Section 6 (pallet lock-in, fixture balance, EDD discipline across fixture families) | Scopus: "flexible manufacturing system" AND (dispatching OR "pallet allocation" OR "fixture allocation") |
| 3 | Researcher in **virtual commissioning / PLC-in-the-loop or software-in-the-loop testing of manufacturing control** | can judge the validation method (real controller code against simulated Modbus PLCs, time compression, validity criteria) | Scopus: "virtual commissioning" AND (manufacturing OR "production system"), journals JMS, IJAMT, J Manuf Mater Process, Procedia CIRP |
| 4 | Researcher in **manufacturing system design / capacity planning, preferably with experience in emerging or newly industrialising economies** | can judge Section 7 (what-if alternatives, investment rule) and the practical relevance | Scopus: ("manufacturing system design" OR "capacity planning") AND (SME OR "developing countr*" OR "emerging econom*") |
| 5 (optional) | **Industrial practitioner** (FMS supplier or system integrator) with publications | judges plausibility of times, load-station design, pallet-pool layouts | conference papers (CIRP, IFAC MIM, IEEE CASE) with industrial affiliation |

## Conflict-of-interest checks (all must be "no")

- co-author with the candidate or the supervisor in the last 5 years;
- same institution or same funding project;
- involvement in the case factory, its machine suppliers or its system integrator;
- editor of the target journal handling this manuscript.

## Opposed reviewers

Only if there is a documented conflict; leave empty otherwise.
