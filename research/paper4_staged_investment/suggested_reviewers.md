# Suggested reviewers – selection criteria (no names)

No real persons are proposed here. The authors should select 3–5 reviewers who meet the criteria below, check
each candidate's recent publications themselves, and confirm that there is no conflict of interest.

## Profiles to cover (one reviewer per profile is ideal)

1. **Capacity planning / stochastic programming.** Has published in the last 5 years on multi-stage or
   scenario-based capacity expansion, ideally with decision-dependent (endogenous) uncertainty or learning.
   Can judge the formulation (Section 3), the rolling-horizon approximation and the value-of-information analysis.
2. **Real options / flexibility in manufacturing investment.** Works on valuing staged or flexible manufacturing
   investments (real options, flexibility in engineering design). Can judge the economic model and the
   interpretation of the boundary conditions.
3. **Simulation-based optimisation / input uncertainty.** Works on discrete-event simulation with input-parameter
   uncertainty, simulation metamodels or screening models. Can judge the screening model, its validation against
   the DES and the statistical design (common random numbers, paired tests).
4. **Digital twins of production systems with data-driven updating.** Has published on twins that are updated
   with shop-floor data (Bayesian or other calibration), preferably during commissioning or ramp-up.
5. (Optional) **Manufacturing in SMEs / emerging economies.** Empirical or case-based work on FMS or Industry 4.0
   adoption in capital-constrained firms. Can judge the managerial implications (Section 7).

## Exclusion criteria (to state honestly in the submission system)

- Co-authors of any of the authors in the last 3–5 years; members of the same institution or doctoral committee.
- Authors of the companion papers or anyone who has seen the thesis chapter in an advisory role.
- Persons with a commercial interest in the case company or in vendors of the options (machines, MES, AGVs).
- Candidates whose only connection is that they are cited in the manuscript: being cited is fine, but do not
  propose a reviewer *because* the paper praises their work.

## Practical hints

- Prefer reviewers from different countries and at least one from an emerging economy.
- Use institutional e-mail addresses only (journals reject private addresses for suggested reviewers).
- Opposed reviewers may be named only with a factual reason (e.g. direct competition on the same case).
