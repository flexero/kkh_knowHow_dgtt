# Suggested reviewers – selection criteria (paper 3)

No names are proposed here. Real people must be selected by the author from the literature, checked for conflicts of
interest and entered in the submission system. MDPI asks for suggested reviewers who are independent of the authors
[verify the current number requested on the Sensors submission page].

## Profiles to look for

| # | Profile | Why this profile | Where to find candidates |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Researcher in **industrial communication / IIoT for manufacturing** (OPC UA, MQTT, fieldbus, edge gateways) with measurement papers | can judge the polling model, RTT and cycle-time results and the comparison in Table 13 | authors of recent journal articles on IIoT gateways and data acquisition in manufacturing (Sensors, IEEE Trans. Ind. Inform., J. Manuf. Syst.) |
| 2 | Researcher in **digital twins of manufacturing systems** with an applied or SME focus | can judge relevance for twin updating, the ISO 23247 alignment and the SME argument | authors cited in Section 2 (retrofit / low-cost twins, SME twins); corresponding authors of reviews on manufacturing digital twins |
| 3 | Researcher in **virtual commissioning / PLC programming / FMS control** | can judge the toggle-bit handshake, the failure-case analysis (Table 5) and the acceptance tests | authors of virtual-commissioning and brownfield integration papers (IECON, Procedia CIRP, Machines) |
| 4 | Researcher in **performance modelling / queueing / stochastic models of sampling** | can check Eqs. (8)–(12) (Poisson partial expectation, reported-change formula) | applied probability or networked-control researchers who publish on sampling and event detection |
| 5 (optional) | Researcher in **OT security / IEC 62443** | can judge Section 7.3 | authors of OT security surveys for manufacturing |
| 6 (optional) | Researcher from an **emerging economy** working on Industry 4.0 adoption in SMEs | can judge the practical claims for the target context | authors of SME / emerging-economy digitalisation studies |

## Exclusion rules (check every candidate)

- no co-authorship with any author in the last 3–5 years [verify the journal's rule];
- not from the authors' institution(s) or the case company; no supervisor–student relation;
- no involvement in the companion papers (systematic review, Bayesian twin, staged investment, pallet-cell design);
- an active publication record in the last five years on the topic; a verifiable institutional e-mail address
  (MDPI does not accept private e-mail addresses for suggested reviewers [verify]).

## Reviewers to oppose (optional)

Only if there is a genuine conflict (for example a direct competitor of the case company). Give a short reason.
